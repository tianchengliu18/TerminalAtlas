# Command content attribution

Terminal Atlas includes an offline snapshot of 10 English command pages from [tldr-pages/tldr](https://github.com/tldr-pages/tldr) at revision `e9fa68b4b42abaa5bec58a6c7ba87f185a535b6d` (retrieved 2026-10-13). The files are in `handbook/imported-tldr/` and correspond to `pages/common/{ls,cd,pwd,grep,find,cat,du,df,curl,ssh}.md`. Their original pages are licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Copyright remains with the tldr-pages contributors.

The application displays those original English pages with locally curated Chinese titles, short summaries, flag explanations, safety labels, and examples. These additions and this combination are modifications of the upstream pages. Each affected entry links to the exact upstream revision in the Inspector. The original snapshot remains separately readable in this repository.

Other built-in command entries and their explanations are original Terminal Atlas editorial content under the repository's document license. Application source code is under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0, as stated in `LICENSE.md`. These are concise learning aids, not complete man pages. User edits stay in IndexedDB as an overlay and are not silently replaced by content updates. Third-party dependency licenses remain with their authors.

The tldr-pages repository states its pages use CC BY 4.0 and its scripts use a separate MIT license; see its [contribution guidelines](https://github.com/tldr-pages/tldr/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). No tldr scripts or explainshell database content are included.
